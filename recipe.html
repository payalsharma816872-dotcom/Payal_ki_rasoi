Aloo Ki Sabzi

⏱ 25 Minutes
🍽 4 Servings
⭐ Easy Recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 Potatoes
  • 2 Tomatoes
  • 2 Green Chillies
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander Powder
  • Salt to Taste
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves

Cooking Steps

  1. Boil and peel the potatoes.
  2. Heat oil and add cumin seeds.
  3. Add tomatoes and spices.
  4. Add potatoes and cook for 10 minutes.
  5. Garnish with coriander leaves.
  6. Serve hot with roti or puri.
